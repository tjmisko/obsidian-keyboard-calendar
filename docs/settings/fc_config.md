# FullCalendar Configuration

Full Calendar exposes its retained display preferences through native Obsidian settings controls.

## First day of week

The first day of the week defaults to Sunday, but you can change it to Monday, or any other day of the week.

## Default calendar view

Choose Month, Week, Day, or List as the initial desktop view. The fork has no mobile or three-day preference.

## 24-hour time

Ditch AM/PM for 24-hour time.
