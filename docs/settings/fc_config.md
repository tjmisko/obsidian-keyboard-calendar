# FullCalendar Configuration

Keyboard Calendar exposes its retained display preferences through native Obsidian settings controls.

## First day of week

The first day of the week defaults to Sunday, but you can change it to Monday, or any other day of the week.

## Default calendar view

Choose Month or Week as the initial main-calendar view on desktop and mobile. Week remains a seven-day range and scrolls horizontally when its columns do not fit. Day is a separate, fixed view in Obsidian's right sidebar or mobile drawer. There is no separate mobile or three-day preference.

## 24-hour time

Ditch AM/PM for 24-hour time.
