# FullCalendar Configuration

Keyboard Calendar exposes its retained display preferences through native Obsidian settings controls.

## First day of week

The first day of the week defaults to Sunday, but you can change it to Monday, or any other day of the week.

## Default calendar view

Choose Month or Week as the initial main-calendar view. Day is a separate, fixed view in Obsidian's right sidebar. The fork has no List, mobile, or three-day preference.

## 24-hour time

Ditch AM/PM for 24-hour time.
